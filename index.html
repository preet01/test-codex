How to play

  • Use the arrow keys or WASD to steer. Tap the arrows below on touch.
  • Eat neon orbs to grow longer and earn points.
  • Speed ramps as you score. Crashing into walls or your tail ends the run.
  • Press Space to pause/resume. Enter or Restart to reset anytime.
